Five questions before every move

Between 400 and 800, most games are lost by leaving a piece where it can be taken. This checklist is worth more rating points than any opening.

  1. What did their last move attack, and what did it stop defending?
  2. Do I have a check, a capture or a threat? Look at checks first.
  3. After my move, what are their checks, captures and threats?
  4. Is the square I am moving to attacked? Count attackers and defenders.
  5. If nothing is going on: which of my pieces is not playing yet?

Your Italian on one card

Always
e4, Nf3, Bc4. Centre pawn, knight with a threat, bishop at f7.
3...Bc5
c3, d3, castle, Re1, then the knight tour b1, d2, f1, g3. Push d4 when everything is ready.
3...Nf6
Ng5. If they recapture on d5 with the knight: Nxf7, Qf3+, Nc3, d4.
...Na5
Bishop check on b5, take on c6, bishop home to e2, knight back to f3. You are a pawn up.
3...Nd4
Take the knight. Never the e5 pawn.
Ng5?
Only when a black knight on f6 blocks the queen's view of g5.

Principles, in order

Each move, stop at the first one that applies. The Guide in Spar does this for you.

  1. Safety. Is anything of mine about to be taken?
  2. Free material. Did they leave something loose? Checks first.
  3. King safety. Castle. Keep the pawns in front of your king still.
  4. Activity. Give your least useful piece a job.
  5. Attack the king when it has lost its cover. Bring pieces, avoid trades.
  6. Trade when ahead. Swap pieces, not pawns.
  7. Open lines. Rooks on open files.

Before you push a pawn

Pawns never go back, so check three things.

  • Does it open a line or win space for a piece that is ready to use it?
  • What square does it stop guarding?
  • Is it supported, or will it become a target?
  • In front of your own castled king: almost never.

Opening habits

  • Move each piece once before you move any piece twice.
  • Castle by move 8.
  • Keep the queen at home until the minor pieces are out.
  • Until you castle, f2 is as weak as their f7.
  • When their king is stuck in the middle, open the centre and keep pieces on.
  • An extra pawn with all your pieces developed is a winning position at this level.

A weekly plan to 800

  • Play the Italian in every game as White. Repetition is what makes it intuitive.
  • Play 10-minute or 15+10 games, not blitz. You need time to run the five questions.
  • Do 10 to 15 minutes of easy puzzles a day. Easy and fast beats hard and slow.
  • After every loss, find the first move where you lost material, and which question would have caught it.
  • Run one Drill session here before you play, and Spar when you want practice with the blunder check on.
  • As Black against 1.e4, answer 1...e5 and develop the same way: knights out, bishop out, castle.
